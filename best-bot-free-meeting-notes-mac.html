The best bot-free meeting note apps for Mac in 2026

Updated November 25, 2026 · Written by the maker of Dayglass

A bot-free note taker records your Zoom, Meet or Teams call from your own Mac, so no extra participant joins the meeting. The main differences are where your notes are stored, whether your AI coding tools can read them, and what it costs. We make Dayglass, so we have said plainly where another app is the better pick.

Quick picks

  • Notes that stay on your Mac and feed Claude Code or Cursor: Dayglass.
  • A polished cloud notepad on Mac, Windows and phones: Granola.
  • Transcribing audio files you already have: MacWhisper.
  • Recording your whole day, on Mac, Windows or Linux: screenpipe.
  • Team sharing and CRM sync in the cloud: Otter or Fathom.
  • Open-source and on-device: Meetily or Anarlog (formerly Hyprnote).

How to choose

1. Decide where your notes should live

Cloud apps such as Granola, Otter and Fathom make sharing and phone access easy, and the vendor stores your transcripts. Local apps such as Dayglass, MacWhisper, screenpipe, Anarlog and Meetily keep transcripts on your computer, which suits client work, legal or health conversations, and anyone who does not want another account holding their calls.

2. Check whether your AI tools can read the notes

If you use Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Cursor, an MCP server lets the agent read your meetings directly, so you stop pasting notes into chat. Dayglass also exposes the call while it is running, so you can ask an agent to plan a change the client just asked for.

3. Match the platform

Dayglass needs an Apple silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later. If you also work on Windows or Linux, look at screenpipe, Granola or Fathom.

4. Look past the free tier

Most free plans cap minutes or history. Dayglass keeps capture, transcripts, search and export free after your first 10 meetings; Pro is for Ask AI over older meetings, AI briefs and the live link.

Questions

What is the best bot-free meeting note taker for Mac?

It depends on where you want your notes. If you want them to stay on your Mac and be readable by Claude Code or Cursor during the call, Dayglass fits. If you want a polished cloud notepad that works on phones and Windows too, Granola fits. If you only need to transcribe audio files, MacWhisper fits.

Which meeting note apps keep transcripts on my Mac?

From the tools on this page, Dayglass, MacWhisper, screenpipe, Anarlog (formerly Hyprnote) and Meetily keep transcription on your device by default. Granola, Otter and Fathom store notes in their own cloud.

Which meeting note apps work with Claude Code and Cursor?

Dayglass and screenpipe run local MCP servers. Granola and Fathom offer hosted MCP servers, and Anarlog and Meetily list MCP support. Dayglass is the one on this list that lets an agent read the call while it is still happening.

Do I need a meeting bot to record Zoom on a Mac?

No. Dayglass, Granola, MacWhisper and screenpipe record without a bot. Otter and Fathom join as a bot by default but also offer bot-free desktop recording.
