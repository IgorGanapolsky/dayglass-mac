Your meetings, readable by Claude Code and Cursor

Updated November 25, 2026

After a client or team call, you usually retype what was decided before Claude Code or Cursor can help. Dayglass records the call on your Mac without a bot and runs a local MCP server, so your agent reads the transcript itself, even while the meeting is still going.

How it works

  1. Install Dayglass: download the DMG, or run brew install --cask igorganapolsky/dayglass/dayglass.
  2. Connect your agents: run dayglass doctor --install-mcp. It adds the local Dayglass MCP server (python3 -m dayglass mcp) to Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Cursor.
  3. Join your Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams call as usual. No bot joins and nobody sees an extra participant.
  4. Ask your agent. During the call it can use dayglass_get_live_meeting to read the speaker-tagged transcript, who is on the call and the shared screen. After the call it can search past meetings.

Things to ask Claude Code

  • "Read the call I'm on and write the implementation plan for what we just agreed."
  • "What did the client say about the deadline in today's meeting? Open a ticket for it."
  • "Turn the action items from my 2 PM call into a checklist in TODO.md."
  • "Which meeting last week first mentioned the billing migration, and who raised it?"

Why local matters here

  • The MCP server runs on your Mac. Transcripts, screen text and notes stay in ~/.dayglass; audio is transcribed on the Mac and not kept by default.
  • Your agent only sees meeting text when it calls a Dayglass tool. If you use a cloud model for Ask AI, the question and the relevant meeting text are sent to that model, and terms on your Never-share list are withheld.
  • No meeting bot means no "Notetaker has joined" moment with clients.

Questions

Which agents can read my meetings?

Claude Code, Claude Desktop and Cursor connect with dayglass doctor --install-mcp. Other tools that speak MCP can run the same local server with python3 -m dayglass mcp.

Can Claude Code read a call while it is still happening?

Yes. The local tool dayglass_get_live_meeting returns the speaker-tagged transcript, the people on the call and the shared screen while the meeting is in progress.

Does a bot join my Zoom, Meet or Teams call?

No. Dayglass records the call audio on your Mac, so nobody sees a bot in the participant list.

Is the Claude and Cursor link free?

Your first 10 meetings include it, no card. After that the live link is part of Pro, $19 a month or $149 a year.

Where is my data stored?

Transcripts, screen text and notes stay on your Mac in ~/.dayglass. Audio is transcribed on the Mac and not kept by default. If you turn on a cloud model for Ask AI, the question and the relevant meeting text are sent to that model. By default, only terms on your Never-share list are withheld before text goes to a cloud model you choose. With a local model nothing leaves your Mac.
