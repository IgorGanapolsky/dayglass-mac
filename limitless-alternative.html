Meta acquired Limitless in December 2025 and stopped selling the Pendant. If you used Limitless or Rewind to remember your meetings, Dayglass does that on the Mac you already have, with no pendant and no bot in your calls.

What Dayglass does

  • Records Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls without a bot joining.
  • Transcribes on your Mac. Audio, screen text and the database stay on your Mac.
  • Reads the text on your screen with Apple Vision, so you can find what you saw.
  • Lets Claude, Claude Code and Cursor ask it questions over MCP: "What did we decide in my last call?"
  • Works on macOS 13 or later. Install from the DMG or with brew install --cask igorganapolsky/dayglass/dayglass.

Do I need a pendant or a bot with Dayglass?

No. Dayglass captures your Mac's audio and microphone during Zoom, Meet and Teams calls, so nothing joins the meeting and there is no extra device. Transcripts stay on your Mac.
