Quick answers

Is this the real app?

It is an illustration of the real features, with fictional names and companies. The app records on your Mac, writes the notes on your Mac, and runs a local MCP server that Claude Code and Cursor connect to.

Do the other people on the call see a bot?

No. Dayglass records from your Mac's system audio and microphone. Paste the 1-click consent note into the meeting chat so people know you are taking notes, and follow the recording laws where you and they are.

Where does the meeting data go?

Transcripts, screen text and notes stay in ~/.dayglass on your Mac. Audio is transcribed on the Mac and not kept by default. If you pick a cloud model for Ask AI, your question and the relevant meeting text go to that model.

What is free and what is Pro?

Your first 10 meetings include everything. After that, recording, transcripts, search and export stay free. Pro ($19 a month or $149 a year) adds Ask AI on older meetings, AI briefs and the live link for Claude and Cursor.
