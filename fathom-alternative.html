What Dayglass does

  • Records Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls without a bot joining.
  • Transcribes on your Mac. Audio, screen text and the database stay on your Mac.
  • Reads the text on your screen with Apple Vision, so you can find what you saw.
  • Lets Claude, Claude Code and Cursor ask it questions over MCP.
  • Works on macOS 13 or later. Install from the DMG or with brew install --cask igorganapolsky/dayglass/dayglass.

How much does it cost?

Capture, transcripts, search and export are free after your first 10 meetings, and those first 10 include every Pro feature, no card. Pro is $19 a month or $149 a year and adds Ask AI across all past meetings, AI meeting briefs, and the Claude and Cursor live link. A founding lifetime license is $199 for the first 100 buyers. There is a 14-day money-back guarantee.

Questions

Does Dayglass use a bot like Fathom's notetaker?

No. Dayglass records from your Mac's audio, so nobody sees a bot in the participant list.

Does Dayglass store my meetings in the cloud?

Audio, screen text and the database stay on your Mac in ~/.dayglass. If you turn on a cloud model for Ask AI, the question and the relevant meeting text go to that model after emails, phone numbers and secrets are swapped for placeholders. With a local model nothing leaves your Mac.

Is Dayglass a team tool?

Dayglass is built for one person's private memory on one Mac. Fathom has team plans with shared features; Dayglass does not.

Is Dayglass on the Mac App Store?

Not yet. Download the notarized DMG or install with Homebrew: brew install --cask igorganapolsky/dayglass/dayglass.
