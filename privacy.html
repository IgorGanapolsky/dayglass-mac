Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026. Dayglass is made by Igor Ganapolsky. This page covers the Dayglass app for Mac and this website.

What stays on your Mac

Dayglass records meeting audio, transcribes it, and reads the text on your screen with Apple Vision. All of that is processed on your Mac. Audio is deleted after transcription by default; transcripts and screen text are stored in a local SQLite database at ~/.dayglass/dayglass.sqlite. We have no server that receives your recordings, transcripts, screen text or notes. Windows of password managers such as 1Password and Bitwarden are skipped during capture.

The app listens only on your own Mac (127.0.0.1) for its window and for tools such as Claude or Cursor that you connect over MCP. It does not accept connections from other computers.

What can leave your Mac, and only if you turn it on

  • Ask AI with a cloud model. The built-in answer engine is Apple's on-device model. If you add your own API key for a cloud model, your question and the relevant meeting text are sent to that provider under your account and its terms. By default, only terms on your Never-share list are withheld before text goes to a cloud model you choose. Other text, such as company and project names, is sent as written.
  • Tools you connect. If you connect Claude, Cursor or another assistant to Dayglass over MCP, that assistant can read what you ask it to search, and its own privacy policy applies to what it does with it.
  • Product analytics. The app can record usage events, such as opening the pricing page, in your local database. They are not sent anywhere unless analytics forwarding is configured, and they never include audio, transcripts or screen text. Set DAYGLASS_ANALYTICS_DISABLED=1 to keep them local in every case.

Licenses and payments

Pro licenses are checked on your Mac, without a network call. Payments are handled by Stripe or Gumroad. We receive your email address and the purchase record from them so we can send your license and handle refunds. We never see your card number.

This website

dayglass.io is hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, which log standard request data such as IP address and browser. The site uses PostHog to count page views and clicks on pricing and download buttons, including the campaign tags in the link you followed. We use this only to understand which pages work. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

Everything Dayglass captured is in ~/.dayglass/ on your Mac. To erase it, quit Dayglass and delete that folder. For a copy or deletion of purchase records, email igor@dayglass.io.

Recording consent

You are responsible for following the recording laws where you and the other participants are. Dayglass includes a one-click consent note you can paste into the meeting chat.
