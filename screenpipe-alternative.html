When to pick which

  • Pick screenpipe if you want everything you do all day recorded, you work on Windows or Linux, or you want it to find workflows to automate across your apps.
  • Pick Dayglass if your calls are where the decisions happen, you are on a Mac, and you want Claude Code or Cursor to pick up the decisions from a call and turn them into a plan, without re-explaining the meeting.

What Dayglass does

  • Records Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams calls without a bot joining.
  • Transcribes on your Mac. Transcripts, screen text and notes stay on your Mac; audio is not kept by default.
  • Reads the text on your screen during meetings with Apple Vision, so you can find what was shared.
  • Lets Claude, Claude Code and Cursor read the call over MCP while it is happening. How the Claude Code link works.
  • Works on macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. Install from the DMG or with brew install --cask igorganapolsky/dayglass/dayglass.

Questions

Is Dayglass a screenpipe clone?

No. screenpipe is built to record your whole computer day. Dayglass is built around meetings: it records calls without a bot, writes notes, and gives Claude Code and Cursor the live call over MCP. Screen capture runs only during meetings unless you turn on all-day capture.

Can I use Dayglass and screenpipe together?

Yes. Both are local MCP servers, so Claude or Cursor can have both connected at once.

Does Dayglass run on Windows or Linux?

No. Dayglass is Mac only, macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon. If you need Windows or Linux, screenpipe supports them.

Where is my data stored?

Transcripts, screen text and notes stay on your Mac in ~/.dayglass. Audio is transcribed on the Mac and not kept by default. If you turn on a cloud model for Ask AI, the question and the relevant meeting text are sent to that model. By default, only terms on your Never-share list are withheld before text goes to a cloud model you choose. With a local model nothing leaves your Mac.
